## Violations in the proposed text

1. **Theory offered before its premises exist.** `m5_theory_initial` (existing node) fires at `groups_completed.gte: 2` and lets the player choose `theory_staging` ("Qualcuno ha disposto alcuni elementi") with zero required evidence about the ring's placement — `m5_cmp_ring` (the only node that formulates P3A) is `"mandatory_beat": false`. A valid path exists where the player answers "disposizione" to Truman having never made the one comparison that would justify it. Bible §5 rule: *"Clues always precede the reveal... The UI must not anticipate deductions."* Report itself flags this at §3.1–2 and §9 ("comparison mandatory before the theory"); fix is already scoped (A9 becomes mandatory, A10 fires only after A9's commit) but is not yet implemented in JSON.
2. **`east_route_confirmed` is committed by dialogue, not by the trace.** `m5_report_close` (mandatory) sets `east_route_confirmed` purely from Hawk's line at the car door ("Dal cartello in poi il sentiero non serve altre proprietà"); no node requires the player to walk to a north-cut landmark and see the tracks pass the car. Per the Fact & Knowledge Ledger's own discipline (row 4), the *rendered acquisition* of "someone left north past the car" should precede or coincide with the state write that opens OEJ; today the state write happens first (or without) the walk. Report §5D(ii) and §12 row A14 both describe the intended fix (a `PROPOSED tracks_north` landmark that must be visited); not yet built.
3. **Jacques's material claims have no antecedent clue.** In all three `m6_interrogation_*` branches, Jacques asserts facts about the car (cards/bank in `prova`'s implicit framing and explicitly in `pressione`/`falsa`; the stove in `falsa`) that no M5 node currently establishes — `E_CARTE` and `E_STUFA` do not exist anywhere in `M5.json`. This is exactly the bible's "assertion, not a clue" failure (§5 rule 3) and the report's own §3.4 finding ("M6 claims what M5 never showed"). It also weakens the tactic choice at `m6_tactic` into a blind guess (no way for the player to prefer `falsa_sicurezza` because they saw the dealer's cut, as report §3.4 notes) and makes the tactic-informing recall page in map §4 row 1 currently impossible to write (it reads an atom that isn't there).
4. **Ronette's fled-toward-town direction is asserted, not shown.** `m5_bridge` places Cooper and the sign at the bridge but the only page confirming "toward town" is implicit (James's Act 2 line, off-scene) rather than a bridge-side observation (worn planks, county stake) the player can read directly. Minor version of rule 3 above; report §3.3 and §5D(i) name the fix (`E_PONTE_DIREZIONE`, not yet built).
5. **Duplicate "none return" claim, no incremental information.** `m5_bridge`'s existing Hawk line already states "Portano a est, nessuna torna indietro" — the *same* content the report proposes to re-deliver at the (not-yet-built) north cut (A14). If A14 is implemented as currently drafted in the report (repeating "none return"), it pays off nothing new beyond "the tracks pass the car specifically," and risks reading as restated exposition rather than a distinct positional discovery — worth tightening the north-cut page to add only the car-specific detail, not restate the bridge's claim.
