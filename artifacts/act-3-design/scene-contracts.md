10. **Channel / limits:** 5 pages max (existing 5 keep; replace `p02` sign text with the stake + worn planks as the rendered acquisition of E_PONTE_DIREZIONE). One PROPOSED Hawk page: refusal to interpret the stake ("La terra la leggo. Il resto lo leggi tu.").
