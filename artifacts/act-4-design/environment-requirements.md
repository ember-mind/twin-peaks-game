## Environment brief — Roadhouse (class C)

Purpose of the place (S3): the one room where the whole town is, so that absence becomes legible: the Palmers are not here; Hawk is on the road; Andy at the station. Spatial requirements (meaning-bearing, in order): (1) the stage at the back where the band plays and where, after the statement, a tall figure stands that nobody looks at (entity tile reachable/faceable from the floor — route-trace to confirm 8,1 vs an adjacent walkable tile); (2) tables along the sides with the townsfolk the player has met (bobby, donna, james, shelly, norma, the Log Lady) — each at a table, none wandering: the room is *full and still*; (3) Truman at a table near the door (the player reaches him first: the actor node); (4) the phone on a wall near the bar (existing target 8,5), visible from Truman's table; (5) the exit door south with the town outside. States: before the statement (band, crowd), after (same crowd, the Giant on the stage, until the phone is used), after the phone (empty of Cooper; never revisited in M8 — a repeat page suffices). Sound: the band that does not stop — if an existing loop/sfx exists, use it; otherwise the caption carries it (no new audio primitive). Art direction: warm interior, the only warm room of the night (the streets and the shore are cold); no red curtains, no dream signals (the Lodge is elsewhere). Parity workflow as `artifacts/traincar-v01/native-notes.md` when produced. NOT this milestone: no concept, no captures.

## Environment brief — the lake shore at night (class C)

Purpose (S6): the first place in the game where Cooper arrives *after* the world; the scene must read as preserved, not discovered. Spatial requirements: (1) the shore tile cluster around 15,28 with the water on one side and the path from town on the other; (2) a perimeter (tape/stakes) visible before the player reaches the tile in the `hawk` version; in the `cooper` version the perimeter is absent and Cooper stops short of the shape (the player's approach stops at a tile before it — the node fires from that tile); (3) the shape on the shore: covered, low, never a face (Bible §8 Maddy: her death never in scene); (4) torches: Hawk and the deputy as entities (present from the start in the `hawk` version; arriving = present after `maddy_trovata` in the `cooper` version); (5) after the act: "Il lago è recintato" — the perimeter stays as the only change to the town map the player can see later (an object-mediated echo). Light: night, one torch source, still water; no moon drama, no red. Do not build: a figure of Maddy, a body sprite with features, water animation systems. Engine: entities with `when` (existing), a town night state (existing pattern), optionally a small placed-object set (perimeter stakes) as sprites — class B/C cosmetic; if the town map cannot carry it, a lot scene on the traincar model is the fallback (class D would be a new map: argued only if the town state fails in review).

## What is deliberately NOT required

- No Palmer house native build for Act 4 (both Act 4 beats there are short/optional; the house's dramatic weight is deferred to the epilogue design).
- No Great Northern lobby work (unused).
- No new map for the crossroads (a tile in front of an existing door).
- No World Time, no weather, no day/night simulation: one authored state today (dusk); a night grade only as a later environment milestone with its own memo tables.
