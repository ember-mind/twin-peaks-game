## S1 — The napkin (diner, afternoon) · FULL · `m8_diner` KEEP

1. **After:** Cooper has met Maddy as a person with a plan (7:40, the lake stop, the switchboard job held until Monday, one more day for Sarah) and has made her one promise; `promise_stance` written. Leland has been visible beside her the whole time, paying.
2. **Wants now:** Maddy — to leave tomorrow on her own terms and to have her name said right once. Cooper — to look at the living: who is near Laura, who is near danger; he does not know he is looking at both. Leland — to see her off "properly" (declared); to keep the day ordinary (protected). Norma — to keep the counter running (refills without asking).
3. **Knows / suspects / falsely believes / withholds:** Maddy knows her plan, nothing of the threat (M8 §3 knowledge map: X on every row). Cooper knows "accadrà ancora" with no referent; suspects a girl near Laura or the house (M8 §4); falsely believes an ordinary afternoon is safe ground. Leland knows everything (truth.md §1) and withholds it behind decorum; declared content: the taxi (S2). Norma knows Maddy's departure question (later: "Ha chiesto della fermata del lago").
4. **Authority / capability:** Maddy owns her departure; Cooper can only offer (accompany / let go / ask for a call); Leland pays the bill — he administers her leaving. Nobody can order anyone.
5. **What changes the moves:** the napkin with three departures (an object she is choosing between); the customer's "Ciao, Laura" (the town's misreading, corrected by her, not by Cooper); the counter (a public place: nothing intimate can be said).
6. **Difference:** Maddy answers with times and institutions (7:40, Spokane, the switchboard, "per iscritto"); Cooper asks with titles ("signorina Ferguson") and measures; Leland speaks in arrangements made on her behalf (S2). Norma speaks through the room (the cup).
7. **Pressure now:** she leaves tomorrow morning; this is the only afternoon; Cooper has an evening appointment (the Roadhouse) and a warning with no address.
8. **Proof of the turn:** the choice itself is performable — Maddy's reply changes what she *does* with the napkin? No: kept as built — the proof is her counter-move to each promise (fixes 7:10 / notes the absence of a "ma" / agrees to call) and, offstage, `promise_stance` being read back by Leland's taxi timing (S2 accompagno page) and at the lake. Deleting the promise changes S2's Cooper line and the lake monologue.
9. **Unsaid, inferable:** the man paying her bill is the uncle who booked her taxi (S2) — the danger is at the counter, not on the road; "quella che porta gli occhiali nelle foto" = she has been mistaken for Laura all week; the switchboard promise "per iscritto" = she has learned not to trust spoken promises (never stated).
10. **Channel / limits:** pages p01–p08 + one feedback page; 3 options; no notebook entry (invariant); Leland as a present, silent entity (staging, no line during S1); the "Ciao, Laura" customer is a page, not an NPC. PROPOSED addition: none to the text. STAGING PROPOSED: Leland entity at the counter beside Maddy from the start (see S1b).

Speakers — **Maddy**: self_image "una che ha un posto che l'aspetta"; declared_content the timetable and the job; leak "l'ho fatto promettere per iscritto" (she does not trust promises), "Uno." (the extra day cost her); protected_truth none — she hides nothing; exit_cost none (she leaves lightly: that is the point). **Cooper**: self_image the investigator who looks after people by method; declared_content polite interest; leak he promises anyway (the stance); protected_truth the warning ("accadrà ancora") — he says nothing of it; exit_cost he leaves without having asked her where she sleeps tonight (never stated; visible later).
