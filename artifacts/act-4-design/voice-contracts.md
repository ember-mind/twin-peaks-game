# Act 4 — voice contracts (speakers with new or rewritten material)

Template: `docs/narrative/templates/voice-contract.md`. A voice is a mechanism (attention · protected value · tactic · disclosure · syntax · politeness · avoided admission · relationship) — never a lexicon. Speakers whose Act 4 material is entirely built and unchanged (Hawk, Norma, the Log Lady, Lucy's built phone line) point to their existing contracts; only Lucy's proposed optional page and Sarah's rewritten page get a block here. Mannerism budget per speaker stated at the end of each block.

## Maddy (built S1/S4 material; contract written now because she is the act's human centre and future stitches will quote her)

Attention: timetables, institutions, what is written down (7:40, Spokane, "per iscritto", "fino a lunedì"). Protected value: her own name and her own morning — a future that is hers, elsewhere. Tactic under pressure: gentle irony that corrects without accusing ("Con due D"; "senza acidità"; the "Ciao, Laura" is answered, not resented). Disclosure: volunteers logistics freely; never volunteers feeling; the feeling leaks through counts ("Uno." — one extra day; "cinque minuti"; "senza un ma"). Syntax: short declaratives with one precise number or noun each; a dash before the correction; turns end on a fact, not a question. Politeness: formal with Cooper ("agente", "lei") but she sets the terms ("Io i federali li faccio aspettare"). Avoided admission: that staying the extra day cost her something, and that she has been mistaken for Laura all week (she says only "quella che porta gli occhiali nelle foto"). Relationship to Cooper: an adult talking to a courteous stranger with a title — never a niece to an uncle, never a witness to an investigator. PATTERN: *she answers a person with a schedule* — "La 7:40 prende la coincidenza a Spokane. Passa alla fermata del lago — quella delle 11 no, ma parte a orario." Budget: zero aphorisms; one correction per scene; never "Laura" as a comparison of herself, only as a fact ("Laura era mia cugina"). Forbidden by lock and Bible patch §8(c): foreboding, fear, "sono come Laura", "non sono Laura" as identity.

## Leland (built S2; PROPOSED S1b one caption)

Attention: arrangements, hours, appearances from outside ("Sarah si tranquillizza se c'è un'auto davanti"). Protected value: the household seen as in order. Tactic under pressure: courtesy that supplies more logistics ("Aspetterà fino alle sette e dieci"). Disclosure: volunteers what a host would (company name, hour); never volunteers where he will be. Syntax: complete, composed sentences; possessives ("il suo accompagnamento"); no dashes, no fragments. Politeness: perfect, unbroken. Avoided admission: everything (truth.md §1). Relationship to Cooper: a notable to a guest of the town. PATTERN: *he administers other people's mornings* — "Ho chiamato la Twin Peaks Taxi: passa da casa alle sette." Budget: no laughter, no dance, no white hair (classic `leland_a4` is dead and must not return: those signals belong to M9/M10 by lock). S1b caption carries no speech.

## Truman (built S3 arrival line, S8 built pages; PROPOSED S8 hook pages)

As Act 3 contract (`docs/act-3-design-report.md` §11): clocks of procedure (stasera / domattina / alle sette), who goes and who stays, what enters the verbale. New material pressure: the loss counted aloud ("oltre a quello che abbiamo perso") — the tactic stays logistic; grief is rendered as dispatch (Andy with Sarah) never as a feeling named. PROPOSED hook: answers a claim with the time and the person who checks it, never with a suspicion of a friend (relationships/truman-leland: "il lutto confonde" is his live belief until M9). PATTERN: *the hour is the plan* — built model: "Andy era già con Sarah quando è arrivata la chiamata." Budget: one clock per turn; no metaphor.

## Cooper (built; PROPOSED S8 hook pages)

As Act 3 contract: measures first, separates the object from its story, names before interpreting. Act 4 pressure: he has no object until the shore; his lines before it are about what he cannot know ("non so ancora cosa si ripeta"; "Nessun fatto ne preferisce uno"). The monologue quotes her, not himself. PROPOSED hook: he reads his own note aloud (an hour written in daylight) and gives it to Truman as the one thing verifiable — no accusation, no "Leland". Aphorism budget: one per scene (p07 is the Roadhouse's; the shore has "Con due D"; the station has none — the hook page must be plain). Forbidden: counting letters; "se fossi arrivato prima"; any evaluation of the night's choices (M8 §14).

## The Giant (built one line)

Not a psychology: a rule. Present tense, no addressee, no direction, no second sentence. The stage entity says nothing on interact (caption only). Never explains (Bible §8). Budget: one statement in the act.

## Sarah (classic `sarah_visione`, page 3 REWRITE)

Attention: the inside of her house (the sofa, the corridor, what was there when she looked). Protected value: to be believed. Tactic: description that stops — her sentences end before the name (facts/sarah-saw-face guard). Disclosure: volunteers the image, withholds the name because she cannot reach it (not because she hides it). Syntax: short past-tense images; the last sentence of the scene breaks off. Relationship to Cooper: the witness the file refuses, to the one agent who writes without arguing. PATTERN (built page 1): "Il divano era vuoto. Poi c'era un uomo accovacciato, come se aspettasse che lo guardassi." Rewritten page 3 specification: the name does not arrive — she knows there is one and stops before it (no "BOB": Ronette is the only source of the name, truman_atto5/Bible). Budget: no scream in scene (M8 §12), no oracle.

## Lucy (built S4 branch; PROPOSED L1 optional page)

As Act 1–3: three sentences, the third a question or a plan; switchboard logic (who is where). PROPOSED L1: the town's evening as a routing fact (where everyone will be, who closes early) — no giant, no Maddy. Budget: one page.

## Speaker-swap probe plan (for the doctrine audit)

Clusters to de-lexicalise: {Maddy, Lucy} (both count and schedule — the difference is that Lucy routes other people and Maddy routes herself); {Truman, Leland} (both give hours — Truman's hours are checks, Leland's are arrangements on someone's behalf); {Cooper, Hawk} at the shore (Cooper names and stops, Hawk reports positions and what he did not touch). The audit attributes by mechanism; failure = a line swaps without changing attention/tactic/disclosure.
