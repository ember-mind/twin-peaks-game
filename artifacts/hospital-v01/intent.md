# Calhoun Memorial — recovery ward (hospital) — scene intent

**Beat.** Act 2, mission M4: Cooper's ten-minute visit to Ronette Pulaski, awake since two in the morning, who cannot address the question and says the name anyway. Gerard in the next bed recites what he never learned. The nurse counts the minutes. This is where the dream becomes physically real: a living witness, a monitor, a bed.

**Function.** One coherent recovery ward, not a hospital: entrance on the south wall from the town, the nurse's work surface between the door and the beds, Ronette's bed as the focal object on the north wall, Gerard's bed second, a curtain divider between them, clear circulation. The player reads entrance → nurse → Ronette without markers.

**Authored state.** Day, cool practical fluorescent light: one flat cool ambient plane, no window drama, no lamp, no warm pool. The single warm/human accent is skin and Ronette's bedding fold; the single small saturated accent is the monitor's trace. Everything else is pale mint/ivory, blue-gray, restrained steel.

**Identity.** Late-1980s small-town American hospital ward: pale institutional mint upper wall with a thin chair rail, cool ivory lower wall, blue-gray linoleum with one quiet darker border band, chrome bed rails, steel bedside stands, a wall-mounted cabinet, a privacy curtain on a ceiling track. Cleaner and brighter than the Sheriff's Station, emotionally colder; nothing shared with the station's forest-green plane or Room 315's walnut and teal.

**Composition (16×12, north wall focal, south entrance).**
- Focal: Ronette's bed, north wall, west of centre, headboard against the wall face; sheets the brightest plane in the room; a bedside monitor on a stand at the head with a small green trace; the actor tile at the foot of the bed for the interaction.
- Secondary: Gerard's bed east of centre, half-hidden by the privacy curtain, a chart at the foot; Gerard beside/at it.
- Nurse's work surface: a low counter or steel cart south-east between the door and the beds, with a chart binder and one clipboard; the nurse stands behind/near it.
- Edge: one wall cabinet on the north wall between the beds, a chair against the west wall, a wall clock. Nothing else.
- Door: double leaf centred on the south wall, small wired-glass panes, straight approach column from the door to Ronette's bed.
- Negative space: at least a 3-tile-wide open floor between the beds and the counter; stillness is the mood.

**Value order.** Ronette's sheets > Gerard's sheets and the curtain > walls > steel > floor. Floor quietest.

**Not wanted.** Blood, grime, decay, horror lighting, flicker, red crosses, modern ICU glass, sci-fi, more than two beds, prop spam, posters, plants, anything that makes the room the threat. The dread is Ronette and the name.
