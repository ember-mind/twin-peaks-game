Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset: ONE finished static pixel-art environment study, Twin Peaks fan RPG, SMALL-TOWN HOSPITAL — RECOVERY WARD, interior, for a top-down RPG. Not a native implementation. Reference image 1 (Sheriff's Station interior) and reference image 2 (hotel room 315) are the project's approved rooms: use them ONLY for camera, native pixel-cluster scale, sprite proportions, wall height, material legibility, contact shadows and commercial finish. Create a completely different room. Do not carry over the station's forest-green walls, oak desks or filing cabinets, nor the hotel's walnut, slate-teal carpet or lamp. Produce the room only, no inset, no comparison sheet, no UI, no labels outside the scene.

Primary request: a small late-1980s American small-town hospital recovery ward, daytime, cool practical fluorescent light. Clinical, quiet, controlled, slightly oppressive, human, believable; unnerving only through stillness. One coherent playable ward with two beds, not a hospital floor.

Style/medium: polished authored pixel art matching the references. Logical canvas 256x192 shown at exact integer 5x (1280x960), landscape 4:3. Crisp chunky square pixel clusters, dark coloured outlines, flat solid colour planes, sparse crisp highlights, hard-edged stepped light. No anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur, no bloom, no painterly noise, no fine tile grain. Flat elevated orthographic RPG camera looking north like the references: upright north wall about three tiles (48 px) tall, furniture tops visible from above, screen-aligned floor axes, no isometric rotation, no vanishing point.

Composition: room fills almost the whole image inside a narrow dark border. North wall is the focal wall. On the north wall, left of centre: a hospital bed with chrome rails, headboard against the wall, white sheets folded down once, a young woman lying under the sheet with her head on the pillow (the patient, the focal figure); at the head of the bed a steel stand with a small bedside monitor showing one thin green trace line, and a drip stand. Right of centre on the north wall: a second identical bed half-hidden by a pale privacy curtain hanging from a ceiling track, a patient's chart clipped at its foot; a middle-aged man in a hospital gown sitting on the edge of that bed, turned toward the wall. Between the two beds on the north wall: one small steel wall cabinet. West wall: one plain chair, a round wall clock above it. East side, toward the door: a low nurse's counter or steel cart with a chart binder and a clipboard, a nurse in a white cap and pinafore standing behind it. South wall cut away, with a centred double door with small wired-glass panes and a straight strip of open linoleum from the door to the first bed. One scale-reference player character standing in the open floor between the counter and the beds, facing the camera, same proportions as the references' player.

Materials and lighting: pale institutional mint upper wall with a thin chair rail, cool ivory lower wall, blue-gray linoleum floor with one quiet darker border band along the walls, chrome rails and steel stands as 2-3 value greys with single crisp highlights, sheets as broad flat white-ivory planes with two or three crease lines, curtain as heavy vertical folds in three pale values, wired glass as cool plane with a faint grid. Exactly one light role: flat cool fluorescent ambient from above (no visible fixtures needed, no window, no lamp, no warm pool); the only warm notes are skin tones; the only saturated accent is the small green monitor trace. Tight 1-2 px contact shadows under every piece of furniture and every figure. Value order: the patient's sheets brightest, then the second bed and curtain, then walls, then steel, floor quietest. Restrained density: about eight readable objects, generous open floor, no clutter, no posters, no plants, no blood, no grime, no red crosses, no medical-prop spam.

Preserve the same game's visual identity above all: it must obviously be the same game as the two references (pixel scale, camera, sprite, cluster discipline) while having its own identity: mint, ivory, blue-gray, chrome, one green trace. Cleaner and brighter than the station, colder in mood. This is one strong composition for visual approval, not photorealism, not 3D, not a vector illustration.
