# Blind native-size review

Two fresh Luna xhigh critics saw only anonymous `option-1`, `option-2`, and approved-reference PNGs in this directory. No code, study history, mapping, or builder rationale. Each judged five criteria at native 1× (0–2 each): silhouette, planes, thickness/contact, material distinction, occupied/empty integration.

| Critic | Option 1 | Option 2 | Winner | One decisive gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 6/10 (1,1,1,1,2) | 10/10 (2,2,2,2,2) | Option 2 | Option 1's broad uniform red backrest flattens upholstered volume. |
| 2 | 7/10 (2,1,1,1,2) | 10/10 (2,2,2,2,2) | Option 2 | Option 1 loses cushion planes and lower contact at 1× despite clearer table geometry. |

Mapping revealed after both verdicts: option 1 = refined R; option 2 = approved production baseline. Lead inspected native frames and agrees with both verdicts. Scores describe image judgments, not an objective measurement of craft. Result: BAR_WINS; static renderer restored to baseline in `4dfb9c8`.
