# Room 315 — scene intent (Phase 1)

**Beat.** End of Act 1: Cooper wakes at 6:20 after the Red Room dream, dressed, in Room 315 of the Great Northern. The player regains control in a real, quiet place. The room must say two things at once: *this is real* (hotel civility, order, morning) and *the dream still matters* (the lamp he fell asleep under is still on; the mirror over the dresser is the Giant's future doorway; the dawn is cold at the window).

**Authored state.** Dawn, 6:20 — a single authored state, distinct from the Town's early dusk and legitimate for the beat. Two light roles: a cool blue-gray dawn plane from the north window (sets ambient and the floor rectangle under the window) and one warm tungsten pool from the bedside lamp (the only warm source: the night was unfinished). Everything else is mid-value walnut and slate-teal.

**Identity.** Upscale-rustic lodge room, late-20th-century American: dark walnut wainscot and door frames, knotty-pine upper wall in a calmer sage-cream, deep slate-teal carpet with one quiet border band, brass fittings. Two hotel-specific details only: the lodge blanket folded at the foot of the bed with a simple geometric stripe, and the brass "315" plate on the hall door. Distinct from the Double R's burgundy/gold hospitality and the Sheriff's Station's cool green institutional plane. Accent family: walnut + slate-teal + amber.

**Composition (16×12 tiles, north wall focal, south wall entrance).**
- Focal: bed with headboard on the north wall, west side; top quilt rumpled, blanket at the foot; bedside table with the lit brass lamp and a black telephone. Cooper wakes standing at the bed's foot.
- Secondary: the north window (broad, low, curtains half-open, cold dawn, pine silhouettes as dark stepped shapes); a small writing desk under the window with the tape recorder and the notebook; the dresser with the oval mirror on the east side.
- Edge props: suitcase on a stand by the east wall, the closed bathroom door on the east wall (background-authored, non-functional), one framed print. Nothing else.
- Hall door: single oak leaf centred on the south wall, brass 315 plate, clear 2-tile approach column from the bed to the door.
- Open floor: at least a 2-tile circulation line from the bed foot to the door and from the door to the dresser; no 1-tile gaps between occluders.

**Value order.** Window plane (cool) and lamp pool (warm) > bed/quilt > desk/dresser wood > walls > carpet. Carpet is the quietest surface.

**Not wanted.** Generic modern hotel, suite scale, decorative overload, owls/stag heads/log-cabin cliché, prop spam, cartoon mystery signage, any second warm source.
