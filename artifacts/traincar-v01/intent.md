# Footbridge / traincar — scene intent (Act 3, M5)

**Beat.** Act 3, first movement: Cooper walks James's road out of town and finds Ronette's bridge and, at the end of the rails, the freight car. The primary scene. Nobody has been inside since the rain. Hawk reads the ground outside; Cooper reads the inside; Truman arrives along the rails for the report; the last daylight of the act ends here. Afterwards the tracks lead north into the trees, toward One Eyed Jacks.

**Function.** First outdoor investigative environment. Three zones on one screen-tall strip, west to east: the footbridge over the creek (town side), the clearing with the car at the dead end of the rails, the north cut where old foot tracks leave the clearing under the trees beside the OEJ sign. The car's interior is drawn roof-cut so that, from its centre tile, the door, the mound just inside it, both torn corners, the crossbeam with the ring and the cold stove are all legible at once. The player reads bridge → rails → car → centre → north cut without markers.

**Spatial facts the picture must carry.**
- Bridge west of the car, on the town side; a county stake with tape on the town bank (where Ronette was found); planks worn on the town half.
- Rails run east from the bridge and stop under the car's east wheels: nothing arrives here.
- Door on the car's south face, one step over a low mound of earth just inside; the mound has a corner of paper showing.
- Interior: torn bench seat south-west corner (damp cards under it), bent sheet-metal north-east corner, a wooden crossbeam across the exact centre with a small ring on it, a small iron stove on the north wall, a grey dust film on the floor unbroken between the corners and the centre.
- Old foot tracks in the ballast pass the car's north-east corner and enter the cut between the trees; the OEJ sign stands beside them; nothing in the cut is the destination, the trail is.

**Authored state.** Late afternoon, overcast, no rain, no sun: flat cool grey daylight, no directional shadows, no dusk grade. One state only. The car is the last daylight scene of the act.

**Identity.** Wet gravel greys, rust and oxidised red-brown of the car, dead-grass ochre, creek slate, the evergreen wall of the station exterior. One warm accent: the ash ring in the stove and the burnt matchbook edge. The county stake's tape is the one pale saturated note outside. Nothing else saturated. Exposed, patient, already read by someone else.

**Composition (24×12, one screen tall, horizontal scroll).**
- Rows 0–1: north canopy face (tree line as wall). Row 11: south tree line. Creek north–south under cols 4–5 with the plank bridge at rows 6–7. Rails along row 7 from col 6 to the car.
- Car: cols 10–17, rows 3–7 (8×5 footprint incl. the 1-tile rim), interior 6×3 walkable (cols 11–16, rows 4–6), door tile at (13,7) south face, mound at (13,6), crossbeam/ring at (13,5)–(14,5) with the ring at (14,5), stove at (15,4) north wall, torn seat/cards at (11,6), bent sheet at (16,4).
- North cut: cols 18–22, rows 2–4, tracks entering the trees at (20,2), sign at (21,3).
- Hawk: (7,6) bridge east end → (12,8) outside the door facing south → (20,3) at the cut facing north. Truman on the rails at (10,8). Player spawn from town at (1,7).
- Negative space: the clearing south of the car (rows 8–10) is open ballast and dead grass; the approach to the door is a straight 2-tile column.

**Value order.** County tape and the car's pale rust highlights > car body and stove ash > gravel/ballast > dead grass > creek slate > evergreen wall darkest. Interior floor dust a quiet pale grey so the untouched centre reads as emptiness.

**Not wanted.** Horror grime, gore, dramatic light, lens perspective, painterly texture, a sunset, rain, a second car, a station, people other than the four sprites, props with no job. The dread is the arrangement, not the place.
