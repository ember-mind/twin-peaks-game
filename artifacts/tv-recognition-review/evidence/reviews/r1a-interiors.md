# Independent interiors recognition review — r1a

## Verdict

**BAR_WINS — overall recognizability 6.3/10** (eight interiors; Palmer day/evening are the same visible room and are scored together). The aspirational 8/10 bar is met by the Double R, Red Room, and Roadhouse. This is a direct visual critique, not a fan-recognition test. `BAR_WINS` means the current capture remains below the 8-point bar; `OURS_WINS` means it meets or exceeds it.

Scores judge place identity, not general art quality. Identity / material-composition / readability are 0–10. Readability means how clearly the room cues survive the small top-down view. Labels, lettering, and named actors are ignored. Recommendations are single, minimal art-direction changes; no art was edited.
