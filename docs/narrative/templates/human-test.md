# Human test — `artifacts/human-tests/<date>-<act>-<tester-id>.md`

PURPOSE: the only source of evidence for fun, natural dialogue, memorability, emotional impact, attachment, suspense. Structural validity is certified elsewhere; nothing here is a gate for freezing structure.
WHEN REQUIRED: before any quality claim leaves "HYPOTHESIS"; recommended once per act.

FIELDS:
- Header: date · build/cache tag · act/span · tester id (no name) · prior exposure · design (within / between subject) · observer.
- Observation log (during, no intervention): hesitation before acting · voluntary exploration · misread objectives · text skipping · twists predicted before they land · "what am I supposed to do?" moments (a finding, not a cue to help).
- Five fixed questions, asked after, in this order, RAW ANSWERS VERBATIM: 1. What were you trying to do? 2. What do you want to know next? 3. What did you expect would happen? 4. What confused you? 5. Which moment or image do you remember?
- Model prediction column (written BEFORE the session) kept separate from the human answer; delta noted.
- Optional discriminating tests, SEPARATE session or cohort only: blind speaker attribution (lines without names; which line proves it) · delayed recall (declare the interval) · choice–cost–echo recall · world-substitution blind A/B (counterbalanced). Raw counts beside every percentage.

PASS: raw answers stored before any interpretation; design and counterbalancing named; no discriminating test mixed into the five-question session.
FAIL: paraphrased answers; the observer helped; a single tester's answer reported as a percentage; a model's self-rating anywhere in the evidence column.

WHAT NOT TO PUT HERE: repairs (go to the next design), praise, interpretation mixed with quotes.
