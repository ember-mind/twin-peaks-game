## 1. Known objective truths (locked; the author never doubts these)

- **The killer.** Leland Palmer killed Laura Palmer at the abandoned traincar. He killed her himself, with his own hands; the confession lock has him say "Io. Al vagone." in all three methods. — source: canon · status: locked · src: Bible §8 (Leland/BOB "[fatti] gli atti"), M8 package §1 ("l'autore (Leland/BOB — verità autoriale, mai scritta a sistema)"), M9–M10 lock §5 B6. → facts/laura-killer
- **The guest.** Within this game's world BOB is real: a presence that has inhabited Leland since childhood ("Avevo dodici anni. C'era una casa bianca… un uomo che chiedeva di giocare"). BOB's influence is real in the project's canon and is never quantified. — source: canon · status: locked · src: Architettura §11, M9–M10 §5 B7b. → facts/bob-guest-since-childhood
- **Fractured responsibility.** The acts are Leland's; the confession shows a discontinuity the game refuses to measure. Leland is perpetrator, instrument and victim at once. This refusal is itself locked: no partition is ever written, not even in this file. — source: project-fact · status: locked · src: Bible §20 decision 7, Grammatica §11 (R7). → facts/responsibility-fractured
- **Maddy.** Leland killed Maddy Ferguson inside the Palmer house, before anyone could arrive, and carried the body to the lake; the letter O was left. Nobody could have intercepted him; no route was the right one. — source: adaptation (series: house; lake and offscreen are project) · status: locked · src: M8 package §1, §8 T2–T6. → facts/maddy-killer, facts/maddy-body-lake
- **The letters.** R (Laura) and O (Maddy) were left by Leland, "un pezzo alla volta"; the cipher diary carries the rule (canonical wording, M8 lock §9-E: «Dice che me lo darà un pezzo alla volta, come le cose che non si possono restituire»; the Act 1 wording «lascerà il suo nome un pezzo alla volta» is deprecated) and the name ROBERT. In game this is a theory until M10 ("R. O. Le ho lasciate io."). — source: adaptation · status: locked · src: Bible §4 E1/E3/E9, §20 decision 2; M9–M10 §5 B6. → facts/letter-r, facts/letter-o, facts/diary-serial-rule
- **Leland's lie.** The taxi for Maddy's first-morning coach was never called; Leland stated it at the diner after the promise scene. The older "Missoula business trip" lie is deprecated. — source: project-fact · status: locked · src: M8 re-lock R12 (2026-08-06), M9–M10 §0 correction lock. → facts/leland-taxi-lie
- **Laura before the game.** She led a double life; she met James and others in places east of town; the traincar was a chosen, repeated place (stove ash of more than one night, cards' wax, planks worn toward town). Before dying she hid the true diary and broke the heart pendant: two deliberate acts of custody, which is why the case is solvable. — source: adaptation · status: locked · src: Bible §4 P1/P2, §8 Laura; act-3-design-report §4. → facts/laura-double-life, facts/laura-hid-diary, facts/laura-broke-heart, facts/traincar-repeated-place
- **The night at the traincar.** Jacques Renault was present and dealt cards; a third man who did not drink watched the stove "come si guarda una persona". That third man is Leland. Jacques did not kill Laura. — source: adaptation · status: locked · src: Bible §6 M6 ("Jacques è l'unico che può collocare il terzo uomo"), §8 BOB, M9–M10 §5 B6 ("Al vagone"). → facts/jacques-present, facts/jacques-not-author, facts/third-man-identity
- **The scene was arranged to be read.** Ring at the exact centre with the dust unbroken to its edge, the ticket "FUOCO CAMMINA CON ME" half buried with one flap showing, no drag marks between the violent edges and the centre. — source: adaptation · status: locked · src: Bible §4 E7/E8, P3; fact-knowledge-ledger rows 1–2. → facts/ring-placed, facts/scene-arranged
- **Ronette.** She survived, fled the car toward town and was found on the footbridge; she saw the face and knows the name; her scream "BOB" is a communicative act. — source: canon · status: locked · src: Bible §8 Ronette; fact-knowledge-ledger row 3. → facts/ronette-fled-town, facts/ronette-scream
- **Jacques's death.** Under county guard, at night, in the hospital, Jacques was smothered (a pillow, per the hospital's report); the register shows no signature between midnight and the shift change. The agent is Leland/BOB, acting because Jacques was the only man who could place the third man. This is author-known and player-hidden: it is never confessed (it is absent from the M10 admissions list by design) and never certified by the runtime (P9 never confirmed). — source: adaptation · status: locked · src: Bible §6 M6, §8 BOB, Architettura §3 timeline Atto 3. → facts/jacques-death, facts/jacques-death-agent, facts/register-no-signature
- **The dream.** In the Red Room Laura whispers the killer's name; Cooper and the player both forget it on waking; both remember the stranger's face. The stranger is the guest, not a resident of the town. That same face is what Sarah saw in her house, and what the dialogue portrait shows during the confession. — source: adaptation (version A) · status: locked · src: Bible §0 conflict 1, §20 decision 1; Grammatica §2. → facts/dream-face, facts/dream-name-lost, facts/sarah-saw-face
- **Sarah.** She perceived first (the vision); she saw the face; she suspected the house. She never "knew". — source: adaptation · status: locked (wording guard [L]) · src: Bible §8 Sarah. → characters/sarah
- **The Giant.** He appears in Room 315 for an established cause (trauma + mirror + the failure of the material method alone) and delivers three statements whose text is canonical in the repository: "È successo di nuovo. E accadrà ancora." · "I gufi non sono ciò che sembrano." · "Senza sostanze chimiche, lui torna." (plus "Questo le apparterrà quando sarà vero"). — source: canon (text) / project-fact (cause) · status: locked · src: Bible §6 M7, §20; Loggia package §1. → facts/giant-three-statements
- **Gerard.** He recites the formula "Fuoco cammina con me"; the ticket at the traincar repeats it textually. The lore of Gerard's own guest is out of scope by decision. — source: canon · status: locked · src: Bible §4 E5/E7, §8 Gerard. → facts/fire-formula, facts/ticket-fire
- **Audrey.** She investigates One Eyed Jacks on her own to count for her father; she is brought back and "read" by Truman; there is no romance with Cooper. — source: adaptation · status: locked · src: Bible §8 Audrey, §6 M11. → facts/audrey-oej
- **Endgame already fixed.** Voluntary interview as an informed person (no warrant); confession under one of the player's methods; Leland dies in the cell; Cooper enters the Lodge awake; Laura is always last; the game's last image is Twin Peaks at dawn. — source: adaptation · status: locked · src: Bible §13, §20; Loggia package §2.
