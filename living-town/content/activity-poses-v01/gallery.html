Activity poses v01 — what an inhabitant is doing, and what hour it is

The production 24px actor cell, Café Meridiana's own material, the native 256×192 canvas.

This is a graphics preview, not a behaviour demo. Every pose below is drawn because this page asked for it by pose id. No inhabitant chooses a pose, no action sets one, and nothing here reads or writes the simulation. Wiring a pose to an activity is not implemented.

Every pose, every frame, one look

Left: native scale, one canvas pixel per game pixel, on Café Meridiana's floor. Right: the same canvas at ×5, nearest-neighbour. The standing walk-atlas sprite opens each row for comparison — same proportions, same outline, same palette slots.

The same pose, all six looks and their apron variants

A pose has to work for every generated inhabitant, in that inhabitant's own colours. Top row: the six base looks. Bottom row: the same six in the apron the view puts on during a shift.

In the café, at native size

Café Meridiana as lt-cafe-scene.js arranges it, painted by the production painter through the shared depth-band pass, with people posed in it. No zoom.

The same room at four minutes of the day

One room, one arrangement, four minutes. The window glass comes from LT.DayLight.at(minute) before the room is painted; the frame is then tinted by LT.DayLight.apply. Nothing is blurred, resampled or scaled.

The day, minute by minute

Every minute of the day as a one-pixel column: window glass on top, the ambient tint under it, the lamp strength as a bar at the bottom. A jump between two adjacent minutes would be a visible seam in this strip.
