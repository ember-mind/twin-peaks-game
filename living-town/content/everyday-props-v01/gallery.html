Everyday props v01 — four objects, drawn by the production renderer

Café Meridiana's own material, the production interior kit, the native 256×192 canvas.

This is a graphics preview, not a behaviour demo. Every frame below is the renderer drawing an object at a spot this page chose. Nobody picks anything up, carries it, reads it, eats it, rides it or repairs it: no inhabitant in Living Town knows these objects exist. Use, lending, riding and repair are not implemented.
