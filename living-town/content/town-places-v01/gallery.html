Town places v01 — the park, the street and the homes

Painted with the production interior kit onto the native 256×192 canvas, over the collision rows in lt-world.js, which are not touched.

This is a graphics preview, not a behaviour demo. Nothing here is wired into the view. The people in these frames stand where this page put them; no inhabitant decided anything, and no simulation is running. Collision is the rows and only the rows — this package paints them and never changes them.

Every place, native 256×192

The same pipeline the view runs: ground layer, then people through the shared depth-band pass with the foreground layer between them, then the day's light over the finished frame. The café is drawn by lt-cafe-scene.js through the production painter, as the bar these are measured against.

The same frames at ×3

Nearest-neighbour, for reading the pixels. Nothing is resampled at native size.

Lamps

Day and night of the same place. Day/night colour is LT.DayLight's; the only thing this package reads the minute for is whether a lantern is burning and whether a window shows a lit sky.
